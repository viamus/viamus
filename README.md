## About me

I'm a software architect working at the intersection of **distributed systems, cloud platforms, supply chain engineering, and artificial intelligence**.

I care about architecture beyond diagrams: how systems behave in production, how teams make technical decisions, and how to turn complexity into reliable, maintainable software.

My approach is pragmatic. I favor clear boundaries, observable systems, engineering autonomy, and automation that solves real problems , not complexity for its own sake.

## What I work on

- **Distributed architecture:** designing and evolving services, integrations, and cloud-native platforms.
- **Engineering enablement:** improving development workflows, technical standards, and developer autonomy.
- **AI-augmented engineering:** exploring coding agents, multi-agent workflows, MCP integrations, and context-aware tooling.
- **Platform reliability:** connecting delivery pipelines, observability, and operational feedback to architectural decisions.
- **Modernization:** evolving legacy and enterprise systems incrementally, with attention to business continuity.

## What I'm exploring

I'm particularly interested in how AI changes the *way we engineer software*, not just how quickly we write code.

That includes agent orchestration, human–AI collaboration, developer tooling, and systems that learn from execution feedback while keeping engineers in control of decisions.

## Engineering principles

> **Strategy beats intensity. Structure beats noise.**

Good architecture should help teams deliver and operate software with confidence. I believe in making decisions explicit, keeping systems understandable, measuring what matters, and choosing the simplest solution that meets the real constraints.
